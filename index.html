What your site is costing you — Semper Fi Heating & Cooling —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a Las Vegas homeowner with a broken AC or a leaking water heater would. Your Las Vegas section is complete and its pages load. The leaks are speed, one after-hours number, and a few signals that point somewhere else.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

The Las Vegas page is very slow in the load test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your Las Vegas page's first content appeared at 12.8 seconds and its largest part, the big background image at the top, at 41.4 seconds. The test also measured 2,400 milliseconds of blocked time while scripts ran, and the page is about 7.5 MB. Your homepage measured 9.0 and 38.1 seconds at about 7.2 MB. One run each.

02

Your booking form sends emergency calls to a 480 number.

The booking form on your Las Vegas page says “For Emergency & After Hours Service please call 480-553-9680”. That is a 480 (Phoenix-area) number. The header of the same page shows 702-818-0284, and the page says you provide 24/7 service. The same form, with the same line, is on every page I saved. I didn't call either number, so I can't say who answers.

03

Your robots.txt points to someone else's sitemap.

The last line of robots.txt is “Sitemap: https://southportheating.com/sitemap_index.xml”. That address is a different website, titled “Plumbing, Heating & Cooling in Madison, WI | Southport”. Your own sitemap isn't named there. Your main sitemap lists 9 pages (plus a separate list of blog posts), and none of the 9 are Las Vegas pages. The Las Vegas pages have their own sitemap at /las-vegas/sitemap.xml (70 addresses), and I didn't find a link to it from robots.txt or the main sitemap.

04

Thank You and 404 pages are listed for Google.

The Thank You page and the 404 page are in your main sitemap and in the Las Vegas sitemap. Both load with a normal 200 response, and their robots tag only says “max-image-preview:large”, so nothing tells Google to skip them.

05

A few titles and descriptions are off.

Your Las Vegas Water Heaters page has no page description and is titled “Water Heaters - Semper Fi Heating & Cooling - Las Vegas”. On the main site, the Contact, Careers and Reviews descriptions are chunks of page text (Contact's starts “Mesa480-933-6990 1335 S Clearview AveMesa, AZ 85209 Tucson…”), and the About description starts with the words “Meta Description:”. The Blog link in your Las Vegas footer goes to a blog titled “Phoenix HVAC & Plumbing Blog”. The Tucson page headline spells it “Tuscon”. The other 56 service, area, coupon and offer pages I opened for Las Vegas all have a description.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your Las Vegas page 86 and your homepage 89. It found 7 links with no readable name, including the logo links, some list markup built from the wrong elements, and a “LEARN MORE” link to the maintenance plan that doesn't say what it is. It flagged no hard-to-read text.

What's working

A complete Las Vegas section: its own address (1111 Grier Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89119), the Las Vegas number 702-818-0284 as a tap-to-call link in the header, Book Now buttons, and a booking form that asks for the service, a date, a time window and details about the unit. All 57 Las Vegas service, area, coupon and offer pages I opened loaded and each has one main heading. Financing, coupons and a maintenance plan are linked in the menu. When I searched your name, your Las Vegas page came first, and the veteran-owned search named you.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the booking and contact forms work, who answers the 702-818-0284 and 480-553-9680 lines, and speed on pages other than the homepage and the Las Vegas page. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.
